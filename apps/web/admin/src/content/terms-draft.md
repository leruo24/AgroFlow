# AgroFlow terms of service

**Draft – needs approval.** Version 0.1-draft, 23 October 2026. This is a working draft written
for the AgroFlow prototype. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer or approved by the AgroFlow team
or Mafikeng Cisco Edge Centre, and it is not yet a binding agreement.

## 1. What AgroFlow is

AgroFlow is an irrigation service for small farms: a field kit that reads the soil and can switch
a pump or valve, a farmer app, and support from AgroFlow staff. The app shows readings, weather
and watering advice, and lets you start and stop watering on zones that have a connected kit.

## 2. What AgroFlow does

- Installs and tests the kit at an agreed visit, and explains how to use it.
- Keeps the app and its advice running, and tells you when it cannot (for example when the kit
  is offline or the weather forecast is unavailable).
- Caps every watering run at your zone's longest-run limit.
- Helps you by phone or message when you ask.

## 3. What you do

- Keep your sign-in details to yourself and choose your own password.
- Keep the kit powered and within range of its network, as explained at installation.
- Check your crops yourself. Advice is a guide worked out from your readings and the forecast;
  it is not a guarantee of yield or of water saved.
- Tell AgroFlow when something looks wrong.

## 4. Limits

- Watering needs the kit, power and a connection. If the kit cannot be reached, a command may not
  arrive; the app says "No response" rather than pretending it worked.
- The kit stops a run by itself at the zone's limit, and if it loses its connection during a run.
  These are safety measures, not a promise that nothing can go wrong with pumps, pipes or valves.

## 5. Cost

The example price shown in the app (R4 000 installation with the hardware for a 2-zone farm, and
R610 a month) is an example only. Your actual cost, or your sponsorship, is agreed with you before
installation. AgroFlow does not take payments in the app.

## 6. Ending the service

You can ask to stop the service at any time. What happens to the kit and your data then is agreed
with you; see the privacy notice for your data.

## 7. Contact

Use Help in the app, or contact the AgroFlow team that installed your kit.
