# AgroFlow privacy notice

**Draft – needs approval.** Version 0.1-draft, 23 October 2026. A working draft for the AgroFlow
prototype, not yet reviewed against POPIA by a lawyer or approved by the AgroFlow team.

## What AgroFlow keeps

- **About you:** your name, phone number, email (if you have one), language and, if you link it,
  your Telegram chat.
- **About your farm:** its name, location (as staff entered or pinned it), zones, crops, planting
  dates, water source, tank size and irrigation type.
- **From the kit:** soil, temperature and humidity readings, watering runs and the kit's status.
- **What you do in the app:** watering requests, alerts you read or dismiss, reports you ask for,
  community reports, and that you accepted these documents (which version, and when).

## Why

To run the service: advice, watering, alerts, support visits and reports. Readings may also be
used, without your name, to improve the advice.

## Who sees it

- AgroFlow staff who support your farm.
- Nobody else sees your name. Community reports are shown to nearby farmers without your name.
- Services AgroFlow uses to run (database hosting, the message service, the weather provider)
  process it for AgroFlow only.

## Your choices

- Telegram, community reports and location permission on your phone are optional and can be
  changed in Settings at any time.
- You can ask what AgroFlow holds about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted.

## How long

While you use the service, and for a period agreed after it ends. Enquiries that did not become a
service can have their personal details deleted.

## Contact

Use Help in the app, or contact the AgroFlow team.
