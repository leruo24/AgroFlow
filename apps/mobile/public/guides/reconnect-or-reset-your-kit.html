Reconnect or reset your AgroFlow kit

Keep this page with the kit. Your kit's own Wi-Fi name and password are on the label on the kit and in the app (Kit tab).

1. Changed your Wi-Fi? Connect the kit again

First open the kit's setup page, in one of three ways:

  • In the app: Kit, then Change Wi-Fi.
  • Or switch the kit off and on 3 times quickly. Each time, switch it off again within 10 seconds.
  • Or wait: after 15 minutes without its Wi-Fi, the kit opens the setup page by itself.

Then:

  1. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi AgroFlow-Setup-XXXX with the password on the label (it starts with AF-).
  2. A setup page opens. If it does not, type 192.168.4.1 in your phone's browser.
  3. Choose your Wi-Fi, type its password and press Save. The setup page stays open for 5 minutes, and opens again later if you miss it.

Your kit stays linked to your farm and keeps watering by its own rules meanwhile. You do not need a new code.

2. Moving the kit to another field or farm

Only AgroFlow staff can do this. Ask them: they make a new enrollment code and connect the kit to its new place.

3. Full reset: only if AgroFlow staff ask you

Switch the kit off and on 5 times quickly. Each time, switch it off again within 10 seconds.

Warning: this wipes the kit's Wi-Fi and unlinks it from your farm. The kit does not water until AgroFlow staff make a new enrollment code and connect it again.

Need help? Open the AgroFlow app and tap Help, or ask AgroFlow staff.
