# AgroFlow walkthrough — design

- Background `#f1f5f9`, surfaces `#ffffff`, text navy `#0f172a`, secondary `#475569`.
- Green `#16a34a` / `#22c55e`: agriculture, healthy, connected, sensor data packets.
- Blue `#0ea5e9`: water, irrigation, cloud/data communication, command packets.
- Amber `#d97706` / red `#dc2626`: warnings and alerts only.
- Inter 400/500/600/700; JetBrains Mono for topic/table names.
- Real screenshots shown unaltered inside neutral device/browser frames; emphasis by
  dimming (spotlight) and hairline highlight rectangles only.
- Motion: power2/power3 eases, 0.4-0.9 s entrances, slow camera pushes, no bounce.
